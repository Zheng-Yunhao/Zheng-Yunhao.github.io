01 / About

From clinical problems
to trustworthy evidence.

I am a postgraduate student at the West China School/Hospital of Stomatology, Sichuan University, under the supervision of Professor Jun Wang. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Dental Medicine from Sichuan University in 2024.

During my graduate training, I have also received methodological training in biostatistics under the mentorship of Professor Xing Zhao in the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at the West China School of Public Health.

My work develops statistical and machine-learning methods motivated by clinically important problems, with an emphasis on rigorous methods, responsible data practices, and interpretable, clinically meaningful evidence.
