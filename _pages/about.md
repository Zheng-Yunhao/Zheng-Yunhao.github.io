I am currently a second-year Master's student at the [West China School/Hospital of Stomatology](https://en.wcss.cn/index.html), [Sichuan University](https://en.scu.edu.cn/).  I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Dental Medicine from Sichuan University in 2024. My research interests include **Biostatistics**, **Computer Vision**, **Orthodontics**, and **Temporomandibular Disorders (TMD)**:

-  **Biopsychosocial Model of TMD**： Utilizing biostatistics and machine learning methods to refine the Biopsychosocial Model of TMD and develop psychometric tools applicable to TMD patients.
-  **Digital Orthodontics**: Development and validation of algorithms and software for digital orthodontic applications.
